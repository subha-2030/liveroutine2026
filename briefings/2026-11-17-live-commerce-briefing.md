# Live Commerce Daily Briefing: Tue, Nov 17, 2026
Focus: what matters for eBay Live

## Top 3 things for eBay Live today
1. **Whatnot is pushing into luxury and cutting fees.** MCM became the first luxury fashion house on Whatnot (Nov 3), and Whatnot added volume-based commission cuts for high-volume sellers, a move widely read as aimed at eBay Live's pricing.
2. **TikTok Shop's LIVE numbers keep rising.** Its UK Live Shopping Report shows live sales up 55% YoY, with 6,000+ sessions a day. TikTok says live sales "more than doubled" in H1 2026. It is running Black Friday LIVE events Nov 13–30 and has shipped Live Auctions.
3. **eBay privacy policy update lands Nov 26.** It adds disclosures about eBay Live data and automated monitoring of livestreams. Expect seller questions.
