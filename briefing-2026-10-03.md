## Top takeaways for eBay Live
- Live shopping is scaling fast in the US. TikTok Shop says live sales more than doubled in H1 2026 vs H1 2025, and says live drives 94% of US luxury resale revenue on TikTok Shop.
- Whatnot is the category leader, reportedly ~60% of North America/Europe live commerce share. ThredUp launched its first Whatnot live show (Sep 30), which is resale inventory competing for the same sellers and buyers as eBay.
- JTV deepened its TikTok Shop partnership: a Sep 28–Oct 4 grand opening, with live programming expanded from 30 to 56 hours. Jewelry and luxury are core eBay categories.
- Big retailers use TikTok Shop live as a launch channel (Boots/No7 live event, Sep 30).
- Amazon Prime Big Deal Days are Oct 6–7 and Walmart Deal Days start Oct 5, so expect heavy promo competition ahead of holiday.
